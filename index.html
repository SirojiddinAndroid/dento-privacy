Dento — Maxfiylik siyosati

Kuchga kirgan sana: 2026-yil 16-noyabr

Dento — stomatologlar uchun kabinet daftari (bemorlar, qabul jadvali, davolash, to'lovlar va laboratoriya buyurtmalari). Ushbu siyosat ilova qanday ma'lumot to'plashi va undan qanday foydalanishini tushuntiradi.

Qanday ma'lumot to'planadi

  • Shifokor hisobi: kirish uchun telefon raqami yoki Google hisobi (ism va email).
  • Shifokor kiritgan ma'lumotlar: bemorlarning ismi, telefon raqami, tug'ilgan yili, tibbiy izoh, qabullar, davolash yozuvlari, tish rasmlari, to'lovlar va laboratoriya buyurtmalari.
  • Telegram bot: bemor botga o'zi ulanganda uning Telegram chat identifikatori (eslatma yuborish va qabulga yozilish uchun).

Ma'lumotdan qanday foydalaniladi

  • Faqat ilova ishlashi uchun: ma'lumotlarni saqlash, qurilmalar o'rtasida sinxronlash, bemorlarga qabul eslatmalarini yuborish.
  • Ma'lumotlar sotilmaydi, reklama uchun ishlatilmaydi va uchinchi shaxslarga berilmaydi.

Qayerda saqlanadi

Ma'lumotlar telefoningizda va Supabase bulut serverlarida saqlanadi. Har bir shifokor faqat o'z kabinetining ma'lumotlarini ko'radi. Kirish kodi SMS orqali Eskiz.uz xizmati, eslatmalar esa Telegram orqali yuboriladi.

Ma'lumotni o'chirish

Hisobingiz va barcha ma'lumotlaringizni o'chirishni quyidagi emailga yozib so'rashingiz mumkin. So'rov 30 kun ichida bajariladi.

Dento — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 16, 2026

Dento is a practice notebook for dentists (patients, schedule, treatments, payments, lab orders).

Data we collect

  • Dentist account: phone number or Google account (name, email) used to sign in.
  • Data the dentist enters: patient names, phone numbers, birth year, medical notes, appointments, treatment records, tooth photos, payments and lab orders.
  • Telegram bot: a patient's Telegram chat ID when the patient connects to the bot themselves (for reminders and booking).

How we use it

Only to run the app: storing and syncing data across the dentist's devices and sending appointment reminders. We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties.

Storage

Data is stored on the device and on Supabase cloud servers. Each dentist can access only their own practice's data. Sign-in codes are sent via the Eskiz.uz SMS service; reminders are sent via Telegram.

Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all data by email; requests are completed within 30 days.
